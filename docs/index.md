# Google Client for Salesforce

Welcome! This is a **free, open-source** solution that replaces Salesforce Files with Google Drive — directly inside Salesforce.

- **Reduce storage costs** — files live in Google Workspace, not Salesforce
- **Native experience** — preview, collaborate, and share without leaving Salesforce
- **Incorporated into business workflows** — folders tied to records, version history, and inline file actions that fit how your teams actually work
- **Secure by design** — if you can't see the record, you can't reach the file
- **Compliance-ready** — full audit logging, fine-grained access policies, no third-party middleware
- **Expanding capabilities** — AI-powered analytics, policy checks against your org's rules, smart labeling, advanced search, and more on the way
